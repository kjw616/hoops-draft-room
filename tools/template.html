About the projections

Stat projections, projected games played, and position eligibility come from ESPN Fantasy for 2026–27. ADP is the FantasyPros consensus of Yahoo and ESPN drafts, which are mostly category leagues, so it can differ from a points league. Δ ADP is ADP minus this page's points-league rank: positive means the points model likes a player more than the crowd does.

ESPN doesn't publish shot attempts, so FGA and FTA are estimated from 2025–26 attempt rates, scaled to projected minutes and reconciled to projected points. They only matter if your scoring uses FGM, FGA, FTM or FTA. Rookies have ESPN projections but no NBA history. Injury tags are ESPN's as of Sep 24 and projected games already reflect ESPN's expectations.
